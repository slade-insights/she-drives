Why women book She Drives

You know who is driving before the day arrives

Most women have had the ride where they text a friend the license plate and keep the phone in their hand the whole way. The apps have started offering a woman driver as a preference, which raises the odds of a match. It does not promise one.

She Drives is the booked version of that. Sherri drives most trips herself, you know her name before she arrives, and the standard does not change because it is late or because the route is unfamiliar.

Every She Drives chauffeur is a woman. If your date, your group size, or your vehicle is something we cannot cover ourselves, we will tell you before anything is booked and let you decide whether a vetted partner operator works for you. We will never put you in a car you did not agree to.

Where we drive

Based in Seal Beach, which turns out to be the useful part

Seal Beach sits on the line between Orange County and Los Angeles County. Long Beach Airport is about seven miles away, John Wayne and LAX are both inside half an hour in normal traffic, and the Long Beach and San Pedro cruise terminals are closer still.

Regular runs from Seal Beach, Los Alamitos, Rossmoor, Long Beach, Huntington Beach, Newport Beach and Irvine, with longer trips to Temecula wine country, Santa Barbara, Palm Springs and San Diego.

Questions people ask

Can I request a woman chauffeur?

Yes, and with She Drives you do not have to request it. Every She Drives chauffeur is a woman, and you know who is picking you up before the day arrives.

Do you charge extra for a woman chauffeur?

No. Safety is not an upgrade, and we do not price it as one. You pay the same rate you would pay anyone else.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Seal Beach and drive throughout Orange County, Long Beach and Los Angeles County, with longer trips to Temecula, Santa Barbara, Palm Springs and San Diego.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. We are a small operation by design, which is what makes the service personal, and it also means popular dates go first.
