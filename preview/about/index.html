About

The Woman Behind She Drives

Sherri Drum founded She Drives in Seal Beach and drives most trips herself. The company exists because of what she wanted as a passenger.

After a traumatic brain injury forced her to rebuild both her health and her working life, she started again with one idea: that getting into a car should not be the part of the day you have to brace for. That applies to everyone, and for women traveling alone it carries more weight.

She Drives is deliberately small. Sherri is usually the person who arrives, which is the whole point. The ambition is to build the bench of women chauffeurs this industry does not have, trained to the same standard, and that part is still being built.

She Drives LLC operates under California TCP 46691-A.
