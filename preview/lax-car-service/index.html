The part that actually goes wrong is the arrival

Departures are easy to plan. Arrivals are where people improvise, and LAX is a bad airport to improvise at: you land late, the terminal is a long walk, and app pickups route you out to the LAX-it lot with a queue and a shuttle at the end of it.

A reserved chauffeur removes that entire sequence. We know your flight number, we watch it, and we have agreed the meeting point with you in writing before you left.

Drive times to LAX, honestly

Everyone publishes the mileage. The useful number is how long the 405 takes at the hour you are actually traveling, so here are typical ranges rather than a best case.

Peak here means roughly 6am to 9am and 3pm to 7pm on a weekday. Friday afternoons run worse than the ranges above, and we plan for that rather than hoping.

LAX questions we get asked

How early should the pickup be for an LAX flight?

From Seal Beach we normally suggest leaving three hours before a domestic departure and three and a half before an international one. That is driven by the 405 rather than by the airport, and we will give you a time for your specific flight when you book.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track the flight, so a delay changes our arrival time, not your plan. You do not need to call us from the plane and you are not charged for sitting in the air.

Where will my chauffeur meet me at LAX?

We confirm the exact meeting point with you before the day, because it depends on your terminal and whether you want a curbside pickup or to be met inside with your bags. You will have the plan and the phone number in writing before you fly.

Can you pick up from a private terminal or FBO?

Yes. Private aviation terminals work differently from the main terminals and we will coordinate directly with the FBO on the arrangements.

Is this cheaper than a rideshare to LAX?

No, and it is not trying to be. A rideshare is cheaper. This is a reserved chauffeur who tracks your flight, meets you by name, handles the bags, and is the same person every time you book.

Do you do the return trip from LAX too?

Yes, and most clients book both at once. Arrivals are the half people most often regret improvising, especially on a late landing.
