Reservations

Reserve Your Chauffeur

Tell us the date, the pickup and where you are headed. We confirm whether we can cover it ourselves and come back with a quote.

What happens next

  1. You send the details below, or call or text if that is easier.
  2. We check availability against the date you need.
  3. You get a reply naming your chauffeur and a quote for the trip. Nothing is booked until you say yes.

Every She Drives chauffeur is a woman. If your date, your group size, or your vehicle is something we cannot cover ourselves, we will tell you before anything is booked and let you decide whether a vetted partner operator works for you. We will never put you in a car you did not agree to.
