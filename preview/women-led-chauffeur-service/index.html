Women-led chauffeur service

Book a Woman Chauffeur by Name

Yes, you can book a woman chauffeur, and with She Drives you do not have to ask. Every chauffeur who drives for us is a woman, and you know which one is coming before the day arrives.

Our promise, including the part most companies leave out

Every She Drives chauffeur is a woman. If your date, your group size, or your vehicle is something we cannot cover ourselves, we will tell you before anything is booked and let you decide whether a vetted partner operator works for you. We will never put you in a car you did not agree to.

We would rather tell you that up front than have you find out at the curb. A reservation only works if the thing you booked is the thing that shows up.

A preference is not a promise

Both major rideshare platforms now let women ask to be matched with a woman driver. It is a genuine improvement and worth using. It is also, by the platforms' own description, a preference that increases your chances rather than a guarantee, and on a busy night you may be matched with someone else.

That gap is the whole reason She Drives exists. A reservation is not a preference. You pick the date, we confirm who is driving, and that is who arrives.

The demand is not imagined either. In a 2026 survey of a thousand American women who use rideshare, close to six in ten said they had felt unsafe in one at least once, and roughly seven in ten said they use a fake phone call when riding alone at night.

Who books us

Women traveling alone

An early flight out of LAX or a late one back. The part of the trip you should not have to think about.

Parents arranging a ride

For a daughter heading to the airport, or for a parent who should not be driving at night.

Companies booking travel

For staff traveling alone, late, or into an unfamiliar city. A visible commitment rather than a policy line.

Anyone who wants the same driver

Weekly meetings, standing appointments, a recurring airport run. The arrangement that gets easier every time.

Who is driving

Sherri drives most trips herself

She Drives is small on purpose. Sherri founded the company after a traumatic brain injury forced her to rebuild her health and her working life, and she built it around the thing she wanted most as a passenger: to feel safe, and not to have to think twice about it.

The goal is to grow the bench of women chauffeurs this industry does not have, training them to the same standard. That part is still being built, and we would rather say so than imply a roster that is not there yet.

Questions women ask before booking

Can I request Sherri specifically?

Yes. Most trips are Sherri. If you want her specifically on a particular date, say so when you reserve and we will tell you straight away whether she is free.

Do you charge more for a woman chauffeur?

No. Nearly half of women say they refuse to pay extra for a woman driver because safety should not be a paid upgrade, and we agree. It is not a surcharge, it is who we are.

What happens if you are already booked on my date?

We tell you before anything is booked. If we cannot cover it with our own chauffeur we will offer a vetted partner operator, whose driver may not be a woman, and you decide whether that works for you.

Can my company set up an account?

Yes. Assistants booking on behalf of someone else is a normal part of the work. Tell us how you need to be invoiced and we will set it up.

Is She Drives a rideshare service?

No. There is no app and no on-demand matching. You reserve in advance, you know who is driving, and the arrangement does not change at the last minute.
